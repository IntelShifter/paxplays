Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

"Pax Plays Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Pax Plays YouTube channel (@PaxPlaysTV) to upload its own videos.

What it accesses. With the channel owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to that channel and read basic channel and video details (title, view and like counts).

What it stores. Only the login token needed to upload, kept as an encrypted secret. It does not collect, sell or share data about viewers or anyone else.

Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of YouTube follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
