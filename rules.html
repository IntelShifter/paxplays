Pax Plays Monthly Robux Giveaway: Official Rules

Last updated November 10, 2026

Each month one commenter wins a $10 Roblox digital gift card. Free to enter.

  1. Who runs it. The Pax Plays channel (@PaxPlaysTV). YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Roblox do not sponsor, endorse or run this giveaway and are released from any responsibility for it.
  2. Who can enter. Anyone 13 or older where giveaways like this are legal. If you are under 18 you need a parent or guardian's permission to enter and to accept the prize.
  3. How to enter. Comment the hidden emoji on any Pax Plays video posted that month (on a long video with three hidden emojis, comment all three). That is the only way to enter, and it is free. Subscribing, liking, sharing or rewatching does not change your chance of winning. One entry per person per video.
  4. Entry period. The first to the last day of each calendar month, Eastern Time.
  5. Prize. One $10 Roblox digital gift card each month. No cash alternative.
  6. Picking the winner. On the 1st of the following month, one comment that names the correct emoji for its video is picked at random from all platforms.
  7. Telling the winner. We reply to the winning comment from the official channel account. The winner has 7 days to respond; after that another winner is picked. We never ask for a password, a payment, or your Roblox login. The code is sent by private message on the platform where the comment was made.
  8. Privacy. We use your public username only to contact you about the prize. We do not collect or keep anything else.
  9. Conduct. Comments that break the platform's community guidelines are not eligible.
  10. Changes. We can end or change the giveaway for future months by updating these rules. A month that has started is always honoured.
